# Task review

Test whether answering the proposed tasks would answer the scientific question within its declared limitations.

## Context

Read `question.md`, `tasks.md`, and relevant terminology or existing result plans. If the question is missing, request it or state that only local task quality can be reviewed. Work only in the identified paper workspace; do not modify framework files.

## Two complementary reviews

- **Top down:** Trace each essential aspect of the question to tasks. Look for unaddressed conditions, mechanisms, alternative explanations, and validation needs. Distinguish a true coverage gap from an intentional exclusion.
- **Bottom up:** Explain how each task's possible answers contribute to the question. Identify peripheral tasks, duplicate questions, ambiguous success criteria, unavailable evidence, and dependencies that prevent an independent answer.

Check conceptual distinctions and inferential scope. For example, synthetic experiments may establish controlled behavior without establishing that it occurs in observations. A missing observational study may require a narrower conclusion rather than a mandatory new experiment.

Prioritize issues by their effect on the scientific answer. For each, state the affected IDs, consequence, proposed resolution, and whether it is a blocker or a refinement. Ask focused questions where a scope or scientific judgment belongs to the researcher.

## Artifact and completion

Record the review in `tasks.md`, including accepted, rejected, and open suggestions with rationale. Apply resolutions supported by the researcher's decisions or existing authorization; keep unresolved consequential changes as proposals. Preserve stable task IDs and explicitly retain excluded or deferred tasks.

If revisions affect existing results, evidence, or the paper map, identify those downstream artifacts as needing review without claiming they have already been updated. Completion means no unresolved blocker remains to explaining how the tasks collectively answer the scoped question; otherwise report the blockers explicitly.
